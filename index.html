Our Story

Calicut's Favourite
Beachside Escape

Tucked on South Beach Road, Kuttichira, EATMOSPHERE was built on a simple belief — food tastes better when the sea is in front of you. From smoky, juicy burgers to fire-grilled steaks and creamy pastas, every dish here is made with real care and real ingredients.

We're the spot locals come back to for date nights, hangouts, Iftar evenings, and lazy afternoon iced lattes. Come as you are — stay for the vibes.
